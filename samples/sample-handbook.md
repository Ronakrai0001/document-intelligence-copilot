# Northstar Studio Team Handbook (fictional sample)

This handbook is fictional sample content for testing Document Intelligence Copilot. It contains no real employee or company information.

## Travel and expenses

Employees should submit travel reimbursement requests within 30 calendar days after the trip ends. Each request must include an itemized receipt and approval from the employee's manager. Finance reviews complete requests within five business days.

Meals during approved overnight business travel are reimbursable up to 45 USD per day. Alcohol, personal entertainment, and minibar purchases are not reimbursable.

## Time off

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests longer than three consecutive working days should be submitted to the manager at least two weeks ahead. The manager confirms approval in the scheduling system.

Unused annual leave does not roll over into the next calendar year unless local law requires it.

## Equipment and security

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen locking. Lost or stolen equipment should be reported to the IT contact as soon as possible, and no later than one hour after discovery.

Employees should store working documents in the approved shared drive. Confidential customer information must not be copied into personal cloud storage.
